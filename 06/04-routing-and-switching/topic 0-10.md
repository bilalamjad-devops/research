### **1. Network ki 5 Main Types** [[00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=34)]

* **LAN (Local Area Network):** Chote area (jaise ghar ya chote office) ko cover karta hai aur is ke links bohat **high-speed** hotay hain [[00:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=49)].
* **WAN (Wide Area Network):** Bare geographic area (shehron aur mulkon) ko aapas mein jorta hai. Isay ISPs (Internet Service Providers) chalate hain aur is ke links ki speed LAN ke muqable mein kam hoti hai [[01:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=88)].
* **MAN (Metropolitan Area Network):** Yeh LAN se bara lekin WAN se chota hota hai aur aam tor par ek hi **shehar (city)** ke andar ek organization ke zariye chalta hai [[02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=138)].
* **WLAN (Wireless LAN):** Is mein users taaron ke baghair (wirelessly) network se connect hotay hain, jis ki sab se aam misal **Wi-Fi** hai [[02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=167)].
* **SAN (Storage Area Network):** Is ka maqsad file sharing aur data retrieval hota hai, jahan high-end servers aur **fiber optic links** ke zariye files store aur distribute ki jati hain [[03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=190)].

---

### **2. Internet Kya Hai?** [[03:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=218)]

Jab mukhtalif LANs, WANs, aur MANs ko routers aur infrastructure machines ke zariye aapas mein jor diya jaye taake ek network se doosre network mein data bheja ja sake, to usay **Internet** ("Network of Networks") kehte hain [[03:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=218)].

---

### **3. Internet Access Technologies (Internet Chalane ke 5 Tareeqay)** [[04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=254)]

* **DSL (Digital Subscriber Line):** Ghar ki dedicated telephone line ke zariye internet chalana [[04:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=262)].
* **Cable:** Cable TV wali taar aur modem (splitter ke saath) ke zariye internet chalana [[04:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=289)].
* **Cellular Data Network:** Mobile towers aur cellular network ke zariye internet istemal karna [[05:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=312)].
* **Dial-up Modem:** Purana aur sasta tareeqa (telephone line par), lekin is ki speed baqi technologies ke muqable mein bohat kam hoti hai [[05:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=325)].
* **Satellite:** Jahan zameeni taaren (landlines) ya infrastructure na ho, wahan satellite ke zariye internet chalaya jata hai (lekin is ki speed bhi kam hoti hai) [[05:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JS3PPYchvQQ&t=347)].
