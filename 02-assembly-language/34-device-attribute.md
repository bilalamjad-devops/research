114. In device attribute word which of the following bit decides whether it is a character device or a
block device
- Bit 12
- Bit 13
- Bit 14
- Bit 15 (Page 166) rep


Sahi Option Hai: **Bit 15**

---

### **1-Line Memory Trick:**

* **Bit 15 = 1:** Device **Character Device** hai (jaise Keyboard, Serial Port).
* **Bit 15 = 0:** Device **Block Device** hai (jaise Hard Disk, Floppy Drive).

---

> **Exam Shortcut:**
> Device Attribute Word ki **sab se pehli / top bit (Bit 15)** hi faisla karti hai ke device **Character** hai ya **Block**!
