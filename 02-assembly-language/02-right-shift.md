Aap ka basic concept aur calculator button dono clear hain. Bas ek chota sa point dhyan mein rakhein:

1. **Button Symbol:**
* **Left Shift:** **`<<`**
* **Right Shift:** **`>>`**


2. **Shift Value (Ginti):**
Question mein jitne shifts likhe honge, aap ko utna number enter karna hai (zaruri nahi ke har baar 2 hi ho):
* Agar **"after two left shifts"** kahe $\rightarrow$ Toh **`<<`** daba kar **`2`** enter karein.
* Agar **"after three left shifts"** kahe $\rightarrow$ Toh **`<<`** daba kar **`3`** enter karein.
* Agar **"after one right shift"** kahe $\rightarrow$ Toh **`>>`** daba kar **`1`** enter karein.



---

### Quick Example Check:

| Question | Calculator Operation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **8 after 1 Left Shift** | `8` $\rightarrow$ `<<` $\rightarrow$ `1` $\rightarrow$ `=` | **16** ($8 \times 2$) |
| **20 after 2 Right Shifts** | `20` $\rightarrow$ `>>` $\rightarrow$ `2` $\rightarrow$ `=` | **5** ($20 \div 4$) |
