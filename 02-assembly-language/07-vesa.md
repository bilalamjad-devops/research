Sahi Option Hai: **High resolution Mode**

---

### **Concept (Samajhne ke liye):**

Old BIOS display standards (jaise standard VGA) low-resolution screen modes support karte thay (jaise $320 \times 200$ pixels). Higher resolution graphics aur zyada colors display karne ke liye VESA association ne ek standard banaya:

* **VESA VBE (Video Electronics Standards Association - Video BIOS Extension):** Yeh ek standard interface hai jo graphics card aur software ke beech kaam karta hai.
* **VESA VBE 2.0:** Yeh specifically **High resolution Modes** (jaise $640 \times 480$, $800 \times 600$, $1024 \times 768$ aur higher color depths) ko access karne ke liye use hota hai.

---

> **Exam Tip:**
> Jab bhi **VESA** ya **VBE** ka zikr aaye CS401 mein, toh hamesha yaad rakhein ke yeh **High resolution Mode** ke liye istemaal hota hai.
