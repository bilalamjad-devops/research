### **Ghari Ki "Tuck-Tuck" Ki Tarah Example**

Aapki deewar wali ghari (clock) har **1 second** ke baad **"Tuck"** karti hai na? Is 1 second ke gap ko hum ghari ka **tick interval** keh sakte hain.

Computer ke andar bhi ek choti si ghari (System Timer) hoti hai. Magar computer boht fast hota hai, toh uski ghari 1 second rukne ke bajaye **boht jaldi "Tuck-Tuck" karti hai**.

* Computer ki ghari ki do "Tuck" (ticks) ke beech mein **55 Milliseconds ($55\text{ ms}$)** ka gap hota hai.
* Is ko **Timer Tick Interval** kehte hain.

---

### **1-Line Summary Rule:**

> **Tick Interval** = Computer ki internal ghari ki do "Tuck" ke darmayan ka time gap (jo ke **55ms** hota hai).

---

> **Bonus Fact:** Is fast speed ki wajah se computer ek second mein lagbhag **18.2 baar** tick karta hai!
