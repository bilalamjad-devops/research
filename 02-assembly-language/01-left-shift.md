### **Windows Calculator (Programmer Mode) - Quick Guide**

1. **1. Open Programmer Mode:** Mode Change.
* Calculator open karein.
* Top-left par **Menu (☰)** par click karke **Programmer** select karein.


2. **2. Select Number Base:** Input Format.
* Left panel se **DEC** (Decimal) par click karein taake normal numbers type kar sakein.


3. **3. Enter the Number:** Initial Value.
* Apna original number type karein (Jaise hamare question mein **`5`** tha).


4. **4. Choose Shift Direction:** Operation Selection.
* **Left Shift** ke liye: **`<<`** button click karein.
* **Right Shift** ke liye: **`>>`** button click karein.


5. **5. Enter Shift Count:** Number of Shifts.
* Kitni baar shift karna hai wo number type karein (Jaise hamare question mein **`2`** tha).


6. **6. Get the Result:** Calculation.
* **`=`** (Equal) press karein.
* Main screen par aapka **DEC** (Decimal) answer aa jayega (Jaise **`20`** aaya).

### **Exam Rules To Remember:**

| Expression in Question | Calculator Button | Mathematical Meaning | Example ($5$) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Left Shift (`SHL`)** | **`<<`** | Multiply by $2$ per shift | $5 \text{ Lsh } 2 = \mathbf{20}$ |
| **Right Shift (`SHR`)** | **`>>`** | Divide by $2$ per shift | $20 \text{ Rsh } 2 = \mathbf{5}$ |
