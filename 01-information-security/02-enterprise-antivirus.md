
## 🟢 A. Enterprise Antivirus

### What is it?

Enterprise Antivirus is installed on **employees' computers or servers** to detect and remove malware.

### DevOps Example

Imagine you're a DevOps Engineer.

Your company has:

* 500 Windows laptops
* 100 Linux servers

You install Microsoft Defender, CrowdStrike, or Sophos on all systems.

Whenever someone downloads a virus:

➡️ Antivirus scans it.
➡️ If it recognizes the malware, it quarantines or deletes it.

### Think Like This

> Antivirus = **Security Guard at the Door**

He checks everyone entering.

But...

If a thief wears a new disguise (new malware)...

The guard may not recognize him.

That's why antivirus isn't enough.
