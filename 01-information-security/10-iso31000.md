### MCQ No 14:

**Correct Answer: D. Level 5**

#### **Concept Samjhen:**

Qualys Vulnerability Scanner vulnerabilities ko unke **impact aur security risk** ke hisab se **Level 1 se Level 5** tak rating deta hai:

* **Level 1 (Minimal):** Informational items (e.g., open ports).
* **Level 2 (Medium):** Low risk information disclosure.
* **Level 3 (Serious):** Sensitive info exposure or misconfigurations.
* **Level 4 (Critical):** High risk, potential remote access or data breach.
* **Level 5 (Urgent / Highest Severity):** Sab se dangerous vulnerabilities jismein attacker bina permission ke **Remote Code Execution (RCE)** kar sakta hai ya full host takeover kar sakta hai.

> **Memory Tip:** Qualys scan mein **1 = Lowest** aur **5 = Highest Severity** hota hai.

ISO 31000 Framework ke bilkul **center / core (markaz)** mein **Leadership and Commitment** hota hai. Top management jab tak direction aur support nahi degi, tab tak risk management poori organization mein implement nahi ho sakti.
