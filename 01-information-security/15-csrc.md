### Correct Answer: **C. NITS resources on computer , cyber, information security and privacy** *(Note: Option mein **NIST** ko typo karke **NITS** likha gaya hai)*

---

### Concept Samjhen:

**CSRC** (Computer Security Resource Center) US Government ki agency **NIST** (**National Institute of Standards and Technology**) ka official security portal hai.

* **NIST CSRC Website:** Is website par cybersecurity guidelines, standards (jaise SP 800-53, Risk Management Framework), publications, aur security baselines milti hain.
* Handouts / MCQ file mein **NIST** ke letters misprint ho kar **NITS** likhe hue hain, isliye Option **C** correct answer hai.

---

### Baki Options Ka Brief Overview:

* **CIS (Center for Internet Security):** Hardening benchmarks aur controls deta hai.
* **SANS Institute:** Cyber security training aur certifications ke liye mashhoor hai.
* **PCI (PCI-DSS):** Payment Card Industry data security standards (Credit card security) set karta hai.

---

> **Exam Tip:** Paper mein agar **NITS** ya **NIST** likha aaye, CSRC (Computer Security Resource Center) hamesha **NIST** ki hi website/project hai!
