**Phishing Email** ek dhoka ya fake email hoti hai jo hacker aapko bewakoof banane ke liye bhejta hai.

Iska naam machli pakadne wale **"Fishing"** se nikla hai—jaise kaante par kaanta (bait) laga kar machli ko phasaya jata hai, waise hi hacker email mein fake daana daal kar aapko phasata hai!

---

### **Phishing Email Kaise Kaam Karti Hai?**

1. **Fake Identity (Jhooti Identity):**
Hacker aapko email bhejega jo dekhne mein bilkul kisi asli company ki lagti hai (e.g., *Google, Netflix, HBL Bank, ya Virtual University*).
2. **Urgency (Darr ya Jaldbazi Paida Karna):**
Email mein likha hoga:
* *"Aapka Bank Account Block ho gaya hai, abhi verify karein!"*
* *"Aapne 1 Lakh ki lottery jeeti hai, click karke claim karein!"*
* *"Password expire ho raha hai, click karke reset karein!"*


3. **Fake Link ya Attachment:**
Email mein ek link hoga. Jaise hi aap us par click karenge, ek nakli login page khulega.
* Agar aap us page par apna **Username aur Password** daal denge, toh wo hacker ke paas chala jayega!
* Ya fir email ke sath koi infected file attached hogi jise download karte hi aapke computer mein **virus/malware** chala jayega (jaise **Anthem breach** mein hua tha).
