### Sawal Kya Pooch Raha Hai?

ISO 27001 (jo security ka international standard hai) ke mutabiq, kisi bhi company ki **Operating Procedures** (kaam karne ke tareeqay / rules) kaisi honi chahiye?

---

### Simple Example Se Samjhen:

Maan lein aap ek bank ya kisi badi company mein kaam karte hain. Wahan system ko restart karne ya password reset karne ka ek security procedure hai.

1. **Agar yeh procedure likha hua na ho (Not Documented):** Har banda apne hisab se kaam karega. Koi ghalti ho sakti hai aur security leak ho sakti hai.
2. **Agar yeh faqat baaton mein ho (Verbally communicated):** Log bhool jayenge ya baat badal jayegi.
3. **Agar bina kisi rule ke hoga (Ad-hoc basis):** Jab jo dil chaha kar liya—jo ke security ke liye sab se bada khatra hai.

---

### Is Liye Sahi Answer "D" Hai:

* **Documented (Likha Hua):** ISO 27001 ka sab se pehla rule yeh hai ke har zaroori procedure **file/document ki shakal mein likha hona chahiye** taake sab ek jaisa aur sahi kaam karein.
* **Available to who need them (Jinko zaroorat ho unke paas maujood ho):** Jis employee ne wo kaam karna hai, uske paas wo guide/document parhne ke liye ready hona chahiye.

> **Aasan Lafzon Mein:** ISO 27001 kehta hai ke *"Jo kaam karna hai usko pehle document (write down) karo, aur jiska wo kaam hai us tak wo document pohanchao."*
