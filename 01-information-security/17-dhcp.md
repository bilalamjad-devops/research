---

### Step-by-Step Kya Hota Hai?

1. **Authentication (Password Check):**
Jab aap Wi-Fi ka password daltay hain, aapka router check karta hai ke password sahi hai ya nahi.
2. **DHCP Request:**
Jaise hi password approve hota hai, aapka phone router se bolta hai: *"Bhai, mujhe network par baat karne ke liye ek IP Address chahiye!"*
3. **DHCP Offer & Assignment (DHCP Service):**
Aapke Wi-Fi Router ke andar ek built-in **DHCP Server** hota hai. Wo aapke phone ko ek dynamic IP address (jaise `192.168.1.15`) assign kar deta hai.

---

### Real Life Result:

Isi DHCP ki waja se aapko apne phone ki settings mein ja kar khud se koi IP address type nahi karna parhta. Sub kuch **automatically** connect ho jata hai!
