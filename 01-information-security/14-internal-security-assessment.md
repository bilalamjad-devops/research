**Internal Security Assessment** (ya Security Assessment) ka matlab hota hai apni hi organization ke IT systems, applications, networks, aur processes ko systematically check aur evaluate karna taake unki **security posture, risks, aur vulnerabilities** ko dhoond kar fix kiya ja sake.

CS205 ke course ke mutabiq iske main points yeh hain:

---

### 1. Definition (Aasan Lafzon Mein)

> **"An effort to assess the security posture, risks, or vulnerabilities for any project, service, application, or device."**
> *(Kisi bhi project, service, ya application ki security ki halat (posture), khadshaat (risks), aur kamzoriyon (vulnerabilities) ko jaanchne aur check karne ka amal.)*

---

### 2. Iski Zaroorat Kab Parhti Hai? (When is it required?)

Course handouts ke mutabiq internal security assessment in soorto-hal mein karwana zaroori hota hai:

* **Launch of a new IT project or service:** Jab koi naya software, application, ya system release hone wala ho.
* **When an incident has occurred:** Jab system par koi cyber attack, breach, ya malware incident ho chuka ho.
* **On change of leadership:** Jab company mein top management, CISO, ya lead change hota hai.
* **Regulatory or compliance requirements:** Audit, legal, ya industry standards (jaise ISO 27001, PCI-DSS) ko poora karne ke liye.

---

### 3. Sequence of Internal Security Assessment (5 Steps)

Paper ke point of view se iska **sequence (steps)** bohot ahem hai:

1. **Management Approval or Communication:** Senior management se permission aur approval lena.
2. **Assign Resources / Roles:** Assessment ke liye sahi team aur tools tay karna.
3. **Build Plan, Scope, and Objectives:** Scope define karna, objectives set karna, aur report format ke mutabiq testing method tay karna.
4. **Conduct Assessment:** Vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, aur auditing perform karna.
5. **Report Findings & Remediation Measures:** Sub bugs/vulnerabilities ki report banana aur unhe fix karne ka plan dena.

---

> **Key Takeaway:**
> External Security Assessment bahar ke hackers/auditors ke perspective se hota hai, jabke **Internal Assessment** organization ki apni security team ya internal auditors karte hain taake launching se pehle hi bugs ko pakda ja sake.
