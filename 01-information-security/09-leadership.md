### Correct Answer: **C. Give credit where it is due**

---

### Concept Samjhen:

Sawal yeh pooch raha hai ke ek **authentic / qabil-e-atebar Information Head (CISO, IT Manager, ya Leader)** ka behavior aur attitude kaisa hota hai?

---

### Ek Acchay Leader / Authentic Head Ki Nishaniyan:

1. **Give Credit Where It Is Due (Dusron ki mehnat ko saraahna):** Ek accha leader team ki kamyabi ka credit khud nahi leta, balkay un team members ko deta hai jinhone mehnat ki hoti hai. Is se team ka morale aur trust build hota hai.
2. **Takes Responsibility for Failures:** Jab koi ghalti ya breach hota hai, toh authentic leader team par malba daalne ke bajaye zimmedari khud leta hai aur mistake admit karta hai.

---

### Baki Options Kyun Sahi Nahi Hain?

* **A. Take credit of everything:** Yeh bad management aur selfish leadership ki nishani hai.
* **B. Never admits mistakes and failure:** Ghalat hai; ahem leader hamesha apni ghaltiyan tasleem karta hai taake improvement ho sakay.
* **D. Very strict and disciplined:** Disciplined hona acchi baat hai, lekin sirf strict hona kisi leader ko "authentic" nahi banata. Authentic leadership ka talluq **integrity aur fairness** se hota hai.

---

> **Key Memory Tip:**
> **Authentic Leader = Fair & Honest** $\rightarrow$ Team ko credit dena aur khud zimmedari lena!
